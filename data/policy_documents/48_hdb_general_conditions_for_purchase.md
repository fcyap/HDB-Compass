1. APPLICATION CONDITIONS AND PROCEDURES

- 1.1 Submission of Application Form

   - (i) You must have a valid HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) Letter to apply for an HDB flat. A non-refundable administrative fee of $10 is payable by credit card (MasterCard / Visa only) or PayNow QR code via your mobile banking app.

To apply for a Community Care Apartment, all applicants must subscribe to and pay for a Basic Service Package under the Basic Service Package Agreement, which must be signed with the service operator appointed by MOH (see Clause 2.4)

- (ii) You and your occupier(s) listed in your new flat application, and your relevant status, must remain the same as those in your HFE letter application. Any subsequent changes, such as changes in household members, marital status, citizenship, ownership of private properties or the status of an occupier to an applicant and vice versa, may affect your eligibility to buy an HDB flat, obtain CPF housing grants and/or take up an HDB housing loan.  Consequently, your HFE letter will become invalid and HDB reserves the right to cancel your new flat application.

- (iii) Any reference to ‘spouse’ in this application refers to both current and late spouse, unless specifically stated otherwise. All declarations and all eligibility conditions for the flat purchase apply to the current spouse (if any) and late spouse (if any).

- (iv) You and any family member listed in the application can only submit one application under this exercise. If the applicant or any member is included in more than one application, all the applications will be disqualified.

- (v) You can only apply for one town/estate and flat category (except for Open booking of flats, where applicants are not required to indicate the town/estate and flat category).

- (vi) If you have earlier booked a flat from HDB or an Executive Condominium (EC) unit from a developer but wish to participate in this exercise, you must cancel your booking before submitting a new application under this exercise (subject to current eligibility conditions) with a valid HFE letter. The usual forfeiture for such cancellation will apply, and the applicants and the core members will not be eligible to apply for another flat from HDB within one year after cancellation of flat booking.

- (vii) Under the Build-To-Order Exercise (BTO) and Sale of Balance Flats Exercise (SBF), all applicants who have a chance to book a flat based on their balloted queue number will be deemed successful in their flat application.

- (viii) Any request for cancellation of an application for this BTO or SBF exercise or change of priority schemes must be submitted and received by HDB within the application period. If the cancellation request is received after the application period, it will still be included in the balloting process and be assigned a queue number, where applicable. If the applicant is shortlisted within 100% of the flat supply, he will not be eligible to participate in subsequent sales launches until after his flat booking appointment and will be treated as one successful application with a non-selection count.  Any ongoing flat applications will also be cancelled.  If the applicant is shortlisted beyond 100% of flat supply and has a chance to book a flat based on his

balloted queue number, it will also be treated as one successful application with a non-selection count.

   - (ix) For applications for a Community Care Apartment, by proceeding with the application, you consent for yourself and on behalf of all persons listed in the application -- the sharing of the (a) personal data of all persons listed in the application and (b) application details (including information submitted for the assessment of priority flat allocation) among HDB, MOH and any service operator engaged by MOH for the purpose of processing the flat application, and planning and administering the programs, activities and services at the Community Care Apartments. Where you provide consent on behalf of another individual listed in the application, you represent and warrant that you have the valid authority and consent to do so.  This consent extends to allowing any service operator appointed by MOH to contact you and all person(s) listed in the application for purposes stated herein.

- 1.2 Points to note for First-timers and First-Timer (Parents and Married Couples) [FT(PMC)] households (Not applicable to Open Booking of flats)

   - (a) An applicant/household is considered a *First-timer applicant/household (and all others considered Second-timer applicants/households) if the applicant(s) and all other core members listed in the Application Form:

      - (i) are not currently owner(s) of a flat bought direct from HDB, a Design, Build & Sell Scheme (DBSS) flat or an Executive Condominium (EC) unit bought from a developer; or

      - (ii) have not sold a flat bought direct from HDB, a DBSS flat or an EC unit bought from a developer; or

      - (iii) have not transferred a flat bought direct from HDB or an HDB resale flat bought under the CPF Housing Grant Scheme; or

      - (iv) have never received any CPF Housing Grant for purchasing an HDB resale flat or a DBSS flat or an EC unit bought from the developer; or

      - (v) have not sold any HDB flat with re-housing benefits under the Selective En Bloc Redevelopment Scheme (SERS); or

      - (vi) have never enjoyed other forms of housing subsidy such as SERS benefits or privatisation of HUDC estate, nor purchased a flat under the Sale of Flats to Sitting Tenants Scheme.

* For married or fiancé-fiancée couples where one party is a First-timer and the other party is a Second-timer, HDB will exercise flexibility to treat these couples as First-timer households in the balloting process and under the priority schemes. These couples will enjoy the same First-timer privileges listed under 1.2(c) if the couple comprises two Singapore citizens or one Singapore citizen and one Singapore permanent resident. They are similarly subject to the terms mentioned in 1.2(d) and 1.2(e).

The above is not applicable for couples who had remarried or are remarrying each other after their divorce.

- (b) A household is considered an FT(PMC) household if the family:

- (i) consists of a ^married couple with at least one Singapore citizen child who is below 19 years old at time of HFE application, or a ^married couple aged 40 and below; and

- (ii) has never owned, had an interest in or sold a residential property before; and

- (iii) has not had a chance to book a BTO/SBF flat in the past five years prior to their current flat application.

FT(PMC) households will receive an additional ballot chance on top of the two ballot chances for first-timer families and enjoy higher priority in BTO and SBF exercises.

^The couple must be married at the time of flat application.

- (c) First-timer families, including FT(PMC) households, will be given more chances than the Second-timer families in the balloting, subject to the prevailing policy on ethnic limits and quota of flats available for booking.

- (d)  First-timer families, including FT(PMC) households, who have a chance to book a flat based on their balloted queue number will be deemed successful in their flat application.  All additional chances accumulated from previous unsuccessful applications starting from Oct 2024 sales launch will be considered used up. This will also apply to towns where HDB has grouped certain flat types together for offer under the same Flat Application Category, such as 5-room and Executive Flats.  First-timer applicants who do not book a flat after one successful application will not be able to enjoy the privileges given to First-timers for a period of one year when they participate in subsequent sales exercises.

During the one-year period, if the First-timer family, including FT(PMC), continues to turn down another chance to book a flat, they will continue to have the First-timer privileges removed for a further period of one year.

- (e) First-timer families whose First-timer privileges are removed as mentioned in 1.2 (d) above will not enjoy the following when they apply for a flat in any HDB sales exercise within the said period (including the extended period mentioned in 1.2(d)):

   - (i) 2 chances in the computer ballot;

   - (ii) Additional chances for subsequent applications for Standard flats if the First-timer families do not have a chance to book a flat in 2 BTO attempts for Standard flats;

   - (iii) Higher proportion of flats set aside for First-timer families who apply under the Family Care Scheme (Proximity);

   - (iv) Family and Parenthood Priority Scheme (Please see clause 1.3 for more information on the Family and Parenthood Priority Scheme.)

Note :

Clause 1.2c to 1.2e does not apply to short-lease 2-room Flexi flat and Community Care Apartment First-timer families who have an interest in private property, locally and overseas, (including those who have disposed of one within 30 months before the date of HFE application).

- # From the October 2024 sales launch onwards, new BTO projects will be classified as Standard, Plus, and Prime based on their locational attributes:

(i) **Standard flats** will come with the standard subsidies and standard restrictions that are applied to all BTO flats. They will continue to form the bulk of the housing supply.

(ii) **Plus flats,** a new category of flats, will be in choicer locations within each region across Singapore (e.g. near MRT station, town centre). These flats will come with more subsidies and tighter restrictions, compared to the Standard BTO flats.

(iii) **Prime flats** are in the choicest locations within Singapore, usually closer to the city centre. They will come with the most subsidies across the three categories and will have the tightest conditions. These flats were previously offered under the PLH model.

- 1.3 Applications under the Family and Parenthood Priority Scheme (Not applicable to Open Booking of flats and applications for Community Care Apartments)

(For First-timer households only)

To qualify for the Family and Parenthood Priority Scheme (FPPS):

- (a) (i) You and your spouse must be first-timer buyers.

   - (ii) A Singapore citizen child (natural offspring from legal marriages or legally adopted) who is below 19 years old at the time of HFE application; or

   - (iii) An expectant mother who will be delivering a Singapore citizen child and declare the particulars of the expectant mother in the Application Form; or

   - (iv) A new mother who was expecting at time of flat application and had given birth to a Singapore citizen child and declared the particulars of the new mother in the Application Form

[Note: During flat booking, the expectant/ new mother who applies and is shortlisted under the FPPS must provide (i) a doctor’s certification of the pregnancy and undertake to provide the citizen child’s birth certificate within six months from delivery, or (ii) provide the birth certificate of the citizen child (if the child is already delivered before the flat booking appointment) before she can proceed to book a flat.]

   - (b) You and your occupiers listed in the Application Form must form a family nucleus; and

   - (c) You and your occupiers listed in the Application Form must meet all other eligibility conditions in buying an HDB flat.

   - (d) Under the FPPS, HDB will set aside up to 40% of the total BTO flat supply, or 60% of the total Sale of Balance Flats flat supply, for public applicants.

   - (e)  FT(PMC)s will be accorded first priority under FPPS if they apply for 4-room or smaller BTO Standard flats. They will similarly be accorded first priority if the town applied for offered combination of standard and plus projects.

- 1.4 Points to note for Second-timer Applicants, households who have taken two housing subsidies and private property owners (not applicable to Open Booking of flats)

   - (a) If you have a chance to book a flat based on their balloted queue number, we will treat this as one successful application.

   - (b) If you do not book a flat after one successful attempt, the household will not be allowed to participate in any HDB sales exercises for one year. Applicants under the Singles Schemes will not be allowed to participate in subsequent sales exercises for one year under the Single Singapore Citizen Scheme, Joint Singles Scheme and Non-Citizen Spouse Scheme.

The terms in 1.4 (a) and (b) apply to the following households:

      - (i) where the applicant(s) and the other core members have taken one or two housing subsidies.

      - (ii) applicant(s) or any person listed in the Application Form or their spouse(s) have an interest in private property, locally or overseas, (including those who have disposed of one within 30 months before the date of HFE application).

- 1.5 Waiver of non-selection count Your non-selection count will be waived if there are 10 or fewer BTO flats to choose from; or 5 or fewer Sale of Balance Flats to choose from when you are invited to book a flat.

- 1.6 Submission of subsequent applications

   - (a) If you applied for a flat under the BTO/SBF exercise and received a queue position that is within 100% of the flat supply, you will not be eligible to apply for a flat in subsequent BTO/SBF exercises until after your flat booking appointment.

   - (b) Upon incurring a non-selection count in a BTO/SBF exercise, HDB will cancel all your existing applications for BTO/SBF exercises from Oct 2023 onwards.

- 1.7 Correspondence Address of Applicants If the correspondence address you have provided in the Application Form is not the residential address on your NRIC or MyInfo profile, and you wish to change

it subsequently, please access e-Service in MyHDB Page via My Flat > Application Status > Check Application Status for New Flat Purchase > Registration Number > **“** Change of Contact Information for Sales Application”.

- 1.8 Changes in Flat Application You are required to notify HDB of any changes in the information provided in the HFE letter/ Application Form and/or of any changes affecting the eligibility to buy an HDB.

Any person listed in the flat application who is single at point of application for HFE letter/ flat application and subsequently married, is required to inform HDB and include the spouse in the flat application.

- 1.9 Payment for flat

   - a) You will need to make the following payments:

|Point of Payment|Item(s) payable|
|---|---|
|(i) During flat Booking|Option Fee of from $500 to $2,000 (based on the type of flat booked), before HDB grants an Option to Purchase|
|(ii) Before Signing Agreement for Lease|Downpayment, related stamp duty and conveyancing fees to the appointed solicitor before the expiry of the Option|
|(iii) Before taking possession of flat|Resale levy (for second-timer applicants) Balance purchase price, related stamp duty and conveyancing fees to the appointed solicitor Required payment for the Basic Service Package (for Community Care Apartments)|

- b) HDB will notify the applicants via SMS/email when the payments are due. Payments will be made via the payment modes specified by HDB in the SMS/ email notification.

- c) If full payment is not received when due (e.g. before the appointment to take possession of flat), HDB reserves the right to cancel the reserved appointment.

- d) With the cancellation of the appointment by HDB, HDB will refund the monies received without accrued interest.  For any cash payment received, HDB will make the refund to the applicants via Interbank GIRO based on the proportion and bank account details provided by applicants.  For any CPF payment received, HDB will make the refund to your respective CPF account(s).

- 1.10 Application Outcome

The information furnished in the HDB Flat Portal/ HFE letter is based on the data and information provided by the flat applicants and may be subject to change if there is any new data or information, or new policies, which affect their eligibility to proceed with their flat application.

- 1.11 Undischarged Bankrupt

If you are an undischarged bankrupt, prior consent must be obtained from the Official Assignee (OA) or the private trustee, to apply to buy :

- (i) An HDB flat that is bigger than a 5-room/ 3Gen flat

- (ii) Any flat type with a net purchase price of $500,000 or above (including any applicable premium/resale levy and cost of components opted under the Optional Component Scheme, where applicable).

You do not need to seek prior consent form the OA or the private trustee if you are listed as an occupier of a flat.

- 2 FLATS SOLD ON SHORT LEASE

- 2.1 Community Care Apartment

   - The Community Care Apartment (CCA) comes with the following features:

   - (a) Short leases ranging from 15 to 45 years in 5-year increments If you and all co-applicants, including your spouses, are at least 55  years old at the time of HFE application, you can choose to buy the flat on short lease ranging from 15 to 45 years in 5-year increments. The allowable lease period is based on the age of the youngest applicant/spouse at the time of HFE application. The chosen lease must last until all applicants and their spouses reach at least 95 years of age. You will select your lease period during flat booking.

(Please refer to the table in clause 6.5.)

- (b) Supply set aside for priority flat allocation (Applicable to CCA under BTO only)

Part of the public flat supply will be set aside for households with at least 1 applicant or core member who requires permanent assistance with his/her activities of daily living. The number of units set aside will vary across projects.

(Please refer to clause 5.8)

- (c) Joint balloting (Applicable to CCA under BTO only)

Up to 2 households (related or unrelated) can apply for joint balloting and have the chance to book the CCAs together.

(Please refer to clause 5.9)

- (d)  The CCA is extended to the following households:

   - (i) you or the other core members have already taken two housing subsidies (please refer to clause 1.2(a)(i) to 1.2(a)(vi) on the various forms of housing subsidies); or

   - (ii) you or any person listed in the Application Form or their spouse(s) have an interest in private property; or

   - (iii) you or any person listed in the Application Form or their spouse(s) You have within 30 months before the date of HFE application bought, transferred or sold any flat, house, building or land, or any interest in it.

The CCA cannot be resold in the open market or rented out.  If you do not need the CCA, you can return the flat to HDB, and HDB will refund the value of the remaining lease of the CCA.

## 2.2 2-room Flexi Scheme

- The 2-room Flexi scheme comes with the following features:

- (a) At least 40% of the public flat supply set aside for seniors. Your application is considered a senior application if you and all coapplicants, including the spouses, are at least 55 years old at the time of HFE application.

- (b) Flexi lease option:

   - (i) 99-year lease or remaining lease years of a 99-year lease; or

   - (ii) short leases ranging from 15 to 45 years in 5-year increments

The allowable lease period is based on the age of the youngest applicant/spouse at the time of HFE application. The chosen lease must last until all applicants, and their spouses reach at least age 95. You will select your lease period during flat booking.

(Please refer to the table in clause 6.5.)

- (c) The 2-room Flexi Scheme is also extended to the following households: (i) the total average gross income of all persons listed in your application is more than $8,000 but does not exceed $16,000 per month at the time of HFE application; or

   - (ii) you or the other core members have already taken two housing subsidies (please refer to clauses 1.2(a)(i) to 1.2(a)(vi) for the various forms of housing subsidies); or

   - (iii) you or any person listed in the application or their spouse(s) have an interest in a private property; or

   - (iv) you or any person listed in the application or their spouse(s) have at any time within 30 months before the date of HFE application bought, transferred or sold any flat, house, building or land, or any interest in them.

However, households mentioned in 2.2(c) are only eligible to buy shortlease 2-room Flexi flat and must meet the terms mentioned in 2.2(a) and the other conditions of purchase and sale under the Build-To-Order System (BTO) or Sale of Balance flat.

The short-lease 2-room Flexi flats cannot be resold in the open market or rented out.  If you do not need the flat, you can return it to HDB and HDB will refund the value of the remaining lease of the flat.

- 2.3 Owning and having an interest in property Applicants or any person listed in the application who own a private property and are buying a CCA or short-lease 2-room Flexi flat must dispose of all interest in the private property within six months from the date of collection of the keys to the new flat.

Condition 2.3 includes HUDC flats and Executive Condominiums, properties acquired by gift or inheritance, private properties, properties outside Singapore, and properties owned, gained or sold through nominees.

- 2.4    Basic Service Package

   - (a) All CCA buyers must subscribe to the Basic Service Package (BSP) with the service operator appointed by MOH. Details of the package, including the scope of services and charges, will be provided in a BSP Agreement to be signed between the flat applicants and the service operator at key collection appointment.

   - (b) Applicants who do not proceed with the signing of the BSP Agreement and/or fail to pay for the BSP as provided in the Agreement will be deemed ineligible for the purchase and will have their flat application cancelled/Agreement for Lease repudiated. Clauses 12.1, 12.2 and 12.3 for flat booking cancellation will apply accordingly.

## 3. ELIGIBILITY CONDITIONS

## 3.1 Eligible Family Nucleus for 3Gen flat purchase

To apply for a 3Gen flat, you and your occupiers must form a multi-generation family comprising any of the following:

- (a) *you, your spouse and children, if any (from lawful marriages or legally adopted), and your parents/married child(ren), where at least one of the parents/married child(ren) is a Singapore citizen or a Singapore permanent resident;

- (b) you (widowed or divorced) and your children under your legal custody, care and control and your parents/married child(ren), where at least one of your parents/married child(ren) is a Singapore citizen or a Singapore permanent resident.

*Applicable to Fiancé/Fiancée couple.

      - # If the care and control of your child(ren) under the age of 21 is shared with other persons, applicant must obtain the written agreement of these persons before listing the child(ren) in the HFE application.

   - (c) If you are booking a 3Gen flat, your parents/married child(ren) will need to sign a 3Gen undertaking.  The signing needs to be witnessed by an HDB staff either via a video call or in person during the flat booking appointment so that your parents/married child(ren) can acknowledge the conditions of booking a 3Gen flat.

   - (d) The 3Gen flat can only be sold on the open market to eligible multigeneration families. You cannot rent out any bedrooms of the 3Gen flat within the MOP.

4. OTHER CONDITIONS

- 4.1 No withdrawal of Core Member(s)

   - The core member(s) of an HFE application are the applicant(s) and occupier(s) who form the requisite family nucleus to qualify for the flat purchase and any housing and mortgage subsidy.  All core members must remain in the flat application and physically occupy the flat during the minimum occupation period (MOP) after the flat purchase.  The MOP is 5 years for Standard flats and unclassified flats, and 10 years for Plus flats and Prime flats.

- 4.2 Ethnic Classification

   - If you and your spouse/fiancé/fiancée/co-applicants are of the same ethnic group, your household will be classified based on your ethnic group. If either of you are of different ethnic groups, you can request to classify your household under either ethnic group and the option made is final and irrevocable.

If you have a double-barrelled race listed on your Identity Card, only the first ethnic group of your double-barrelled race will be considered. For example, if you have a double-barrelled race of “Indian-Chinese”, only “Indian” will be used in the ethnic classification process. This ensures the same treatment for applicants with singular or double-barrelled race.

Your household’s ethnic classification at the point of flat purchase will remain unchanged when you later sell the flat.

- 4.3 Wait-out Period

   - (a) Cancellation of flat booked

      - If you cancel your application after booking a flat, you and the core members of your cancelled application will not be eligible to apply to buy another flat from HDB within one year after the cancellation.

   - (b) One-year wait-out period if you do not book a flat If you are a Second-timer applicant or first-timer single (including noncitizen spouse scheme) who do not book a flat when successful, you will

not be allowed to participate in the subsequent sales launches, including the Open booking of flats, for a period of one year.

- 5    PRIORITY/ ASSISTANCE SCHEMES (Not applicable to Open Booking of Flats)

- 5.1    Important Notes

You may wish to apply for an HDB flat under one of the following Priority or Assistance schemes. The conditions for these schemes (known as the 'Conditions of the Scheme') are in addition to all of HDB's conditions for purchase. If there is any inconsistency between the Conditions of the Scheme and the General Conditions For Purchase of a flat, the Conditions of the Scheme shall prevail.

You can apply for **only one** Priority Scheme and will be given priority allocation in accordance with HDB’s prevailing policies.

In the event of death, divorce or separation of any person listed in your application after approval of the application under any of the Priority Schemes, you must provide the relevant documents (e.g. Death Certificate, divorce documents, Deed of Separation) to HDB for verification within 14 days from the date of the demise, divorce or separation respectively.

If you become ineligible to buy an HDB flat under any Priority Schemes, your flat application will be cancelled and all monies paid will be forfeited.

If you and/or your occupiers breach any conditions of the Priority Schemes or make any false declaration/ misrepresentation under these Schemes for purchasing an HDB flat, your application will be cancelled and all monies paid towards the application will be forfeited. If you have already taken possession of the HDB flat, HDB will compulsorily acquire or terminate the Lease of the HDB flat and all monies paid towards the flat purchase will be forfeited.

- 5.2 Tenants' Priority Scheme Tenants’ Priority Scheme is restricted to purchasing a 2-room Flexi or 3-room flat only. To be eligible for the scheme, you must meet the following conditions:

   - (a) you must be a First-timer or Second-timer who is currently an HDB rental flat tenant; and

   - (b) you must have a family nucleus; and (c) you must have stayed in the existing HDB rental flat for at least 2 years at the time of your HFE application

- 5.3 Third Child Priority Scheme To be eligible for the Third Child Priority Scheme, you must satisfy all the following conditions :

   - (a) You  and/ or your spouse must be a Singapore citizen.  If you are divorced or widowed, you must be a Singapore citizen to apply.

   - (b) You must have at least 3 children* (from legal marriages or legally adopted) and comply with the following conditions:

- (i) your third child must be born on or after 1 Jan 1987;

- (ii) your youngest child listed in your HFE application must be a Singapore citizen;

- (iii) all your other children listed in the HFE application must be Singapore citizens or Singapore permanent residents

* For divorced parents, you must have legal custody, care and control of your children. For adoptive parents, you must have the Adoption Order of your children.

- (c) At the point of your flat application, if (i) you are expecting a third child , or (ii) you were expecting your third child when your HFE application was approved and the said child (Singapore citizen) has since been born, you are eligible to apply for Third Child Priority Scheme if the particulars of the expectant mother are indicated in the flat Application Form.

## Third Child Priority Scheme applies to families who have fewer than 2 children when their HFE application was approved and are, at the time of their flat application, expecting twins or triplets, or whose twins and triplets have since been born.  Consequently, references to ‘third child’ in clause 5.3 apply equally where the third child is one of the twins or triplets.

[Note: During flat booking, the expectant mother who applies for and is shortlisted under the Third Child Priority Scheme must provide (i) a doctor’s certification of the pregnancy, (ii) undertake to obtain Singapore citizenship for her third child (after the said child has been delivered), and (iii) undertake to provide HDB with a copy of her  third child’s (Singapore citizen) birth certificate within six months from delivery of the said child.

Alternatively, if the expectant mother’s third child was born before the flat booking appointment, she must provide the birth certificate of the said citizen child before she may proceed to book a flat.]

- (d) Each family is only allowed to enjoy the benefit of the Third Child Priority Scheme to buy a flat once.

- (e) You will be given priority allocation in accordance with HDB’s prevailing policies.

- (f) You will become ineligible to buy an HDB flat under this Scheme if the composition of your 3-children family as listed in your application changes by reason of death or divorce or separation or for any other reasons at any time whatsoever.

- (g) As a successful applicant, you must ensure that the 3 youngest children listed in your HFE application are continuously listed in your flat application until key collection and that they reside in the flat during the relevant minimum occupation period (5 years for Standard flats and unclassified flats, 10 years for Plus and Prime flats).

For successful applicants who are expecting their third child (when their HFE application was approved or at the point of flat application), upon delivery of the said third child, he/she must similarly be continuously listed

in your flat application until key collection and reside in the flat during the relevant minimum occupation period (as reflected above).

During this period, you must ensure that these children:

      - (i) do not withdraw as owners/occupiers of your new flat and must physically and continuously occupy the flat;

      - (ii) do not submit any application or be included as occupiers in another application to buy a flat directly from HDB, a resale flat or EC unit; and

      - (iii) do not rent an HDB flat (including DBSS flat)

   - (h) If you are or become ineligible to buy an HDB flat under this Scheme, your application shall be cancelled at HDB’s discretion and all monies paid by you shall be forfeited by HDB.

   - (i) You must comply with all other Terms and Conditions as may be imposed from time to time under this Scheme.

- 5.4 *Family Care Scheme (FCS) (Proximity) You can apply for the FCS (Proximity) if you include your parents/child(ren) in your application to live with you or wish to buy a flat to live near your parents/child(ren), provided you meet the eligibility conditions. Among FCS (Proximity) applicants, you will enjoy first priority if you have included your parents/child(ren) in the application, over other FCS (Proximity) applicants.

To be eligible for the FCS (Proximity), you must meet the following conditions:

- (a) Living with Parents/Child(ren) within the 5-year minimum occupation period (10-year for Plus flats and Prime flats)

   - (i) If you list your parents/married child in your application to live with you, at least 1 parent/married child must be a Singapore citizen/ Singapore permanent resident.  If you list your single child(ren) (including divorced/widowed/separated persons without a child) in your application to live with you, at least 1 single child must be at least aged 35 and a Singapore citizen/Singapore permanent resident.

   - (ii) As a successful applicant, you must ensure that before you take possession of your new flat and for a period of 5 years (10 years for Plus flats and Prime flats) from the date of taking possession of your new flat, your parents/child(ren):

      - do not withdraw as owner/occupier of your new flat and must physically and continuously occupy the flat;

      - do not submit any application or be included as occupiers in another application to buy a flat directly from HDB, a resale flat or an EC unit; and

      - do not rent an HDB flat (including DBSS flat)
