## Home buying guide for members below 55

A home is a long-term commitment. Choosing a right-sized home that suits your household's needs, lifestyle and budget can keep your housing payments manageable over time.

It is also useful to consider how the purchase may affect your retirement savings and whether your loan payments can remain manageable as circumstances change.

Moving involves significant upfront costs (such as stamp duties, legal fees, renovation costs). If you are upgrading to a more expensive flat, you may also need to manage higher loan payments and property taxes.

With more of your income going towards housing, it is useful to consider whether you will still have enough for your daily needs, unexpected expenses and future retirement.

It's important to consider not just your current income but also how it may change in future.

Think whether you can comfortably manage your monthly payments, bearing in mind possible job changes, income fluctuations, and the reduction in contribution that is allocated to your Ordinary Account as you get older. Plan for a home that you can continue to afford over the long term, so it remains a sound decision that supports both your housing and retirement needs.

Having thought through these considerations, if you are ready to proceed with your next home purchase, visit HDB's website to learn more about your eligibility to sell, and apply for an HDB Flat Eligibility letter.

You can use CPF savings for your home's downpayment and loan instalments (or mix with cash). Using CPF savings provides cash flexibility but you'll forgo up to 3.5%\* risk-free interest in your Ordinary Account. Consider how much CPF savings to use today with your future retirement needs in mind.

*\*Subject to terms and conditions*

Check your **Home ownership dashboard** for available savings that you can use from your CPF accounts, as well as the refund requirements upon sale of your current flat.

Sale proceeds from your current flat can be used to finance your next home, but not all may be available. If you're above 55 and haven't met the Full Retirement Sum, part of CPF housing refunds may go to your Retirement Account.

If you are purchasing a resale flat next, use our **Housing Usage Calculator** to estimate your CPF usage based on the remaining lease.

Apply for an HDB Flat Eligibility letter to find out if you can purchase a new or resale flat, as well as the amounts of CPF housing grants and HDB housing loan you are eligible for.

If you are purchasing a second subsidised flat, you may be required to pay a resale levy in cash. Additionally, if you are obtaining a second HDB housing loan, HDB's terms and conditions may apply.

Visit HDB's website to find out more about the resale levy payable and the second HDB housing loan.

Explore a range of flats across different locations, as prices and housing options can vary by area. This may help you better understand the options available and find a home that aligns with your needs, budget and long-term plans.

Visit HDB's website to search for new and resale flats that match your preferences.

Besides the convenience and being surrounded by your loved ones, you also get to enjoy CPF housing grants and schemes provided by the Government to encourage families to live closer together.

Visit HDB's website to learn about the CPF housing grants available and priority schemes that enhance your chances in the balloting of flats.

You generally have up to 6 months after collecting the keys to your next home to sell your current flat. However, if you intend to reuse the CPF housing refunds from the sale for your next home, you will need to sell your current flat earlier and factor in the refund processing time. Please allow up to 15 working days from the date CPFB receives the refunds for the refunds to be credited to your CPF account. Refer to this FAQ for more information on the refund processing time, and to your Home ownership dashboard. for the amount of CPF housing refunds you can reuse.

If you are purchasing a resale HDB flat and intend to reuse the refunds, note that it typically takes about 3 months from exercising the Option to Purchase to key collection. Plan the sale of your current flat so that the refunds are credited before key collection.

HDB's contra facilities allow you to sell your current flat and purchase another flat at the same time, using the sale proceeds and refunded CPF savings to help finance the new purchase.

The Contra Payment Facility applies to new HDB flat purchases, while the Enhanced Contra Facility applies to resale flat purchases. Visit HDB's website to check your eligibility and find out how the facilities may help with cash flow.

Visit HDB's step-by-step guide to find out about the purchasing and selling process.
