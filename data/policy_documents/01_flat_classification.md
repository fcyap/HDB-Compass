# HDB Flat Classification Framework: Standard, Plus, and Prime Flats

**Source URL:** https://www.hdb.gov.sg/residential/buying-a-flat/new-flats/flat-classification
**Effective Date:** 2024-10-01
**Policy Category:** classification
**Flat Classifications:** Standard, Plus, Prime
**Keywords:** Standard, Plus, Prime, MOP, subsidy clawback, whole-flat rental, 10-year MOP, resale income ceiling, October 2024 BTO

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Policy Evolution

On **1 October 2024**, the Housing & Development Board (HDB) and Ministry of National Development (MND) enacted the most significant structural reform to Singapore's public housing system in over three decades. 

### 1.1 Abolishment of the Mature vs Non-Mature Classification
For decades, HDB classified estates as either **Mature** (estates with developed amenities, older flats, and higher prices) or **Non-Mature** (newer developing towns like Jurong West, Punggol, Woodlands). 

This legacy framework became obsolete because:
1. **Islandwide Infrastructure Parity:** Extensive MRT network expansions (Thomson-East Coast Line, Jurong Region Line, Cross Island Line), polyclinics, hawker centres, and shopping malls eliminated the stark infrastructure disparities between mature and non-mature estates.
2. **Limited Undeveloped Land:** Most new public housing projects must now be built in choice locations within established towns or redevelopment precincts (e.g., Bayshore, Mount Pleasant, Greater Southern Waterfront, Pearl's Hill).

### 1.2 The Tripartite Framework (Standard, Plus, Prime)
Starting with the **October 2024 Build-To-Order (BTO) exercise**, all new flats are classified based on their specific locational attributes, accessibility, and market desirability into three distinct categories:
- **Standard Flats:** Built islandwide in good locations. They constitute the vast majority of the housing pipeline and operate under standard housing rules.
- **Plus Flats:** Situated in choice locations within each region, such as immediate proximity to transport interchanges, town centres, or scenic waterfronts (e.g., Bayshore Palms, Bayshore Vista, Central Musgrave in Clementi, Kembangan Wave).
- **Prime Flats:** Situated in prime, central locations with the highest market values and unmatched connectivity (e.g., Crawford Heights in Kallang/Whampoa, Bukit Merah, Queenstown, Greater Southern Waterfront).

> **Important Transitional Note:** Existing HDB flats completed or booked prior to October 2024 remain governed by the rules prevailing at their point of launch. They do not retroactively convert to Plus or Prime designations. Earlier Prime Location Housing (PLH) projects launched between Nov 2021 and June 2024 (e.g. River Peaks I & II, King George's Heights) continue to follow their specific launch terms (which mirror Prime rules with a 6% clawback).

## 2. Comprehensive Classification Comparison Matrix

The table below outlines the ten statutory regulatory dimensions across Standard, Plus, and Prime flats:

| Policy Dimension | Standard Flats | Plus Flats | Prime Flats |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Locational Attributes** | Islandwide good locations | Choice locations within regions (near MRT / town centre) | Prime, ultra-central, downtown enclaves |

## 3. Deep Dive: Prime Flats Regulations

Prime flats represent the pinnacle of public housing convenience and subsidy. To keep these premier flats affordable for ordinary Singaporeans at launch while preventing speculative "lottery effect" windfalls, HDB enforces stringent guardrails.

### 3.1 Additional Market Subsidies & Subsidy Recovery (Clawback)
- **Why Subsidy Recovery Exists:** Prime flats require substantial additional taxpayer subsidies above standard BTO flats to price them within reach of eligible Singaporean households.
- **The Clawback Mechanism:** First-hand owners who sell their Prime flat on the open market after completing the 10-year MOP must refund a fixed percentage of the gross transaction proceeds to HDB.
  $$\text{Subsidy Recovery Amount} = \max(\text{Resale Transacted Price}, \text{Official HDB Valuation}) \times \text{Clawback Rate}$$
- **Clawback Range:** Typically **6% to 9%**, determined and announced at the launch of each individual BTO project (e.g., Crawford Heights launched in October 2024 has an 8% subsidy recovery rate).
- **Subsequent Resales:** Subsidy recovery applies **only to the first resale** by the original BTO buyer. Subsequent resale buyers who later sell the flat do not pay subsidy recovery, because they purchased the flat at fair market resale value without the initial government subsidy.

### 3.2 10-Year Minimum Occupation Period (MOP)
- Owners must physically occupy the flat for a minimum of **10 continuous years** before they are permitted to sell the flat on the open market.
- **Calculation of MOP:** The 10-year period commences strictly from the date of physical key collection. Any period during which the owners do not physically occupy the flat (e.g., extended overseas work or unauthorised subletting) does not count towards the 10-year MOP.
- **Private Property Investment Ban:** During the 10-year MOP, flat owners and their spouses are strictly barred from purchasing or acquiring any interest in local or overseas private residential property.

### 3.3 Lifelong Ban on Whole-Flat Rental
- **The Rule:** Renting out the **entire flat is strictly prohibited for life**, even after the 10-year MOP has been fulfilled.
- **Room Subletting Exception:** Owners may rent out individual spare bedrooms after completing the 10-year MOP, provided that:
  1. The owners continue to physically reside in the flat as their primary residence.
  2. The flat is a 3-room or larger flat (2-room flats cannot sublet bedrooms).
  3. All subtenants are registered with HDB within 7 days of lease commencement.
- **Enforcement & Penalties:** Unauthorised whole-flat rental constitutes an illegal subletting offence under the Housing and Development Act, subjecting the owner to financial penalties or compulsory flat acquisition by HDB.

### 3.4 Stringent Resale Buyer Eligibility Conditions
When a Prime flat is sold on the open resale market, the buyer must satisfy criteria identical to purchasing a brand-new BTO flat:
1. **Citizenship:** At least one buyer must be a Singapore Citizen (SC). A household consisting solely of Singapore Permanent Residents (PRs) cannot buy a Prime resale flat.
2. **Family Nucleus:** Buyers must qualify under the Public Scheme, Fiancé/Fiancée Scheme, or Orphans Scheme.
3. **Singles Access:** Eligible single citizens aged 35 and above can purchase Prime resale flats, but are **strictly restricted to 2-room flats**.
4. **Income Ceiling:** Gross monthly household income must not exceed **$14,000** for families, or **$7,000** for singles.
5. **Private Property Divestment:** Resale buyers must not own any private residential property, and must observe a **30-month wait-out period** after disposing of private property before purchasing a Prime flat.

## 4. Deep Dive: Plus Flats Regulations

Plus flats bridge the gap between Standard and Prime flats. They occupy highly accessible, desirable locations within regional towns (e.g., Bedok South, Clementi, Bayshore, Kembangan).

### 4.1 Subsidy Recovery (Clawback) Rate
- Plus flats receive additional market subsidies, though lower than Prime flats.
- First-hand buyers are subject to a **3% to 6% subsidy recovery rate** on the higher of the resale price or market valuation upon the first resale.
- Example: If a Plus flat in Bayshore is sold on the open market for $900,000 with a 6% clawback, the seller must return $54,000 ($900,000 \times 0.06) to HDB upon completion of sale.

### 4.2 10-Year Minimum Occupation Period (MOP)
- Plus flat owners are subject to the same **10-year MOP** as Prime flats.
- Owners cannot sell the flat or purchase private residential property until the full 10-year physical occupancy requirement is fulfilled.

### 4.3 Lifelong Whole-Flat Rental Prohibition
- Just like Prime flats, **Plus flat owners are never permitted to rent out the whole flat**, even after the 10-year MOP.
- Only spare bedrooms can be rented out after MOP, subject to mandatory owner occupancy.

### 4.4 Resale Market Eligibility Guardrails
Subsequent open-market buyers of Plus flats must meet:
1. At least one Singapore Citizen in the household.
2. Monthly household income ceiling of $14,000 (families) or $7,000 (singles).
3. Singles aged 35+ can purchase 2-room Plus resale flats only.
4. Ex-private property owners must serve a 30-month wait-out period.

## 5. Deep Dive: Standard Flats Regulations

Standard flats remain the bedrock of Singapore's public housing system, accounting for approximately 70% to 80% of new BTO supply across towns like Woodlands, Yishun, Sengkang, Jurong, Bukit Batok, and Tampines.

### 5.1 Baseline Parameters
- **Minimum Occupation Period (MOP):** **5 years** from key collection date.
- **Subsidy Clawback:** **0%**. Owners keep 100% of their net sales proceeds (after CPF principal + accrued interest refund and mortgage settlement).
- **Whole-Flat Rental:** Owners **can rent out the whole flat** on the open market once the 5-year MOP is completed, subject to HDB approval and prevailing Ethnic Integration Policy (EIP) and Non-Malaysian SPR quotas.
- **Open Market Resale Conditions:** When sold on the secondary market, Standard flats are open to all eligible Singapore Citizens and Singapore Permanent Residents without an income ceiling. Singles aged 35+ can purchase any flat size up to Executive/3Gen on the open market.
