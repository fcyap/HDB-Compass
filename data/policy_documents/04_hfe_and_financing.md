## 1. The HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) Letter

The HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter is the mandatory, digital first step for all prospective homebuyers in Singapore. Enacted to streamline the fragmented home-buying journey, the HFE letter consolidates three critical assessments into a single application:
1. **Housing Eligibility:** Confirms eligibility to buy a new BTO flat or resale flat under specific statutory schemes.
2. **Grant Eligibility & Quantum:** Computes exact CPF Housing Grant amounts (EHG, Family Grant, PHG, Singles Grant).
3. **Housing Loan Eligibility & Quantum:** Confirms whether applicants qualify for an HDB housing loan and provides the exact maximum borrowing limit.

### 1.1 Strict Procedural Timelines & Legal Mandate
- **Mandatory Pre-requisite:**
  - **For BTO / SBF Launches:** Applicants **must have a valid HFE letter** before submitting an application during a sales launch.
  - **For Resale Flats:** Buyers **must obtain a valid HFE letter before obtaining an Option to Purchase (OTP)** from a resale flat seller. An OTP signed without a valid HFE letter is null and void under HDB regulations.
- **Validity Period:** The HFE letter is valid for **9 months** from its date of issuance.
- **Two-Stage Process:**
  - *Step 1 (Preliminary Assessment):* Instant online financial indication via My HDBPage.
  - *Step 2 (Formal Application):* Submission of Singpass Myinfo data, latest 3 to 12 months payslips, CPF contribution history statements, and credit bureau reports. Processing typically requires 14 to 21 working days.

## 2. Prudential Borrowing Regulations: MSR, TDSR, and LTV Limits

Housing loans in Singapore are strictly regulated by the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) and HDB under macroprudential guidelines to safeguard financial stability and prevent over-leveraging.

### 2.1 Mortgage Servicing Ratio (MSR) — 30% Ceiling
- **The Rule:** Monthly mortgage repayments for the purchase of an HDB flat must not exceed **30%** of the borrowers' gross monthly household income.
- **Scope:** MSR applies to **all HDB flat purchases**, regardless of whether the loan is taken from HDB or from a commercial financial institution.
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{Maximum Monthly Instalment} = 0.30 \times \text{Gross Monthly Household Income}$$
- **Example:** For a couple earning a combined gross monthly income of $8,000, their maximum monthly mortgage repayment for an HDB flat is strictly capped at $2,400 ($8,000 \times 0.30).

### 2.2 Total Debt Servicing Ratio (TDSR) — 55% Ceiling
- **The Rule:** Total monthly debt obligations across all financial institutions must not exceed **55%** of the borrowers' gross monthly household income.
- **Scope:** TDSR aggregates all personal debts, including:
  1. The prospective property mortgage.
  2. Motor vehicle loans.
  3. Student and personal loans.
  4. Minimum monthly payments on credit card facilities.
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{All Monthly Debt Obligations} \le 0.55 \times \text{Gross Monthly Household Income}$$

### 2.3 Loan-to-Value (LTV) Ceilings & August 2024 Reform
The LTV limit defines the maximum borrowing quantum relative to the property's purchase price or market valuation (whichever is lower).

#### Key Policy Change on 20 August 2024:
To encourage financial prudence and cool market demand, MND and HDB lowered the HDB concessionary housing loan LTV limit from **80% to 75%**, bringing it into complete alignment with commercial bank loans.

#### Comprehensive Loan Comparison Matrix
| Parameter | HDB Concessionary Housing Loan | Commercial Bank Housing Loan |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Maximum LTV Ceiling** | **75%** (lowered from 80% on 20 Aug 2024) | **75%** (for first property loan) |
| **Interest Rate** | Fixed at **0.10% above prevailing CPF OA rate** = **2.60% p.a.** | Floating (e.g. 3M SORA + margin) or fixed packages (~3.0% to 3.8% p.a.) |
| **Minimum Purchase Downpayment** | **25%** of purchase price / valuation | **25%** of purchase price / valuation |
| **Minimum Cash Downpayment** | **0% mandatory cash**. Can be funded **100% using CPF OA funds and CPF housing grants**. | **Strict minimum of 5% in liquid cash**. Remaining 20% can be funded with CPF OA / cash. |
| **Early Repayment Penalty** | **None**. Can make partial or full capital prepayments anytime with zero penalty. | Penalties (typically 1.5%) apply if repaid during the lock-in commitment period. |
| **Maximum Loan Tenure** | 25 years (or up to age 65 of borrowers) | 30 years (or up to age 65 of borrowers) |
| **Income Eligibility Cap** | Household income must not exceed **$14,000/month** ($21,000 extended family) | **No income ceiling** |

## 3. Buyer's Stamp Duty (BSD) Progressive Tax Structure

Buyer's Stamp Duty (BSD) is a statutory tax payable on the execution of all residential property purchase contracts in Singapore, administered by the Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore (IRAS). It is levied on the transacted purchase price or official market valuation, whichever is higher.

### 3.1 Statutory BSD Progressive Slabs
| Purchase Price Portion (SGD) | Marginal Tax Rate | Maximum Tax Payable within Slab |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| First $180,000 ($0 to $180,000) | **1%** | $1,800 |
| Next $180,000 ($180,001 to $360,000) | **2%** | $3,600 |
| Next $640,000 ($360,001 to $1,000,000) | **3%** | $19,200 |
| Next $500,000 ($1,000,001 to $1,500,000) | **4%** | $20,000 |
| Next $1,500,000 ($1,500,001 to $3,000,000) | **5%** | $75,000 |
| Any excess above $3,000,000 | **6%** | 6% of excess amount |

### 3.2 Worked Example: BSD Calculation for an $850,000 Resale Flat
1. First $180,000 @ 1% = $1,800
2. Next $180,000 ($180k to $360k) @ 2% = $3,600
3. Remaining $490,000 ($360k to $850k) @ 3% = $14,700
- **Total BSD Payable:** $1,800 + $3,600 + $14,700 = **$20,100**.
- **Payment Method:** BSD can be paid using CPF OA funds. If CPF funds are insufficient, it must be paid in cash within 14 days of exercising the OTP.

## 4. Cash Over Valuation (COV) in the Resale Market

### 4.1 Definition & Mechanism
Cash Over Valuation (COV) occurs when the agreed resale transacted price exceeds the official market valuation determined by HDB's appointed licensed valuer:
$$\text{COV} = \text{Agreed Purchase Price} - \text{Official HDB Valuation}$$

### 4.2 Strict Financing Rules for COV
- **Zero Loan Financing:** Neither an HDB housing loan nor a commercial bank loan can finance COV. Loan quantum is calculated strictly based on the **lower** of the purchase price or HDB valuation.
- **Zero CPF OA Financing:** CPF OA savings cannot be withdrawn to pay for COV.
- **Mandatory Liquid Cash:** The entire COV amount **must be paid 100% in liquid cash** by the buyer to the seller upon completion of the resale transaction.
- **Strategic Impact:** If a buyer agrees to purchase a flat for $750,000 and HDB inspects and values the flat at $720,000, the $30,000 COV must be paid entirely in cash on top of the standard 5% cash downpayment (if taking a bank loan), BSD, and legal fees.

## 5. Total Upfront Cash & CPF Capital Requirement Formula

When advising home buyers, the total upfront capital requirement is computed as:
$$\text{Total Capital Outlay} = \text{Downpayment (25%)} + \text{BSD} + \text{COV} + \text{Legal/Valuation Fees (~$2,800)}$$
- **HDB Loan Scenario:** Downpayment can be funded 100% from (CPF OA Balance + CPF Housing Grants). Minimum mandatory cash is $\text{COV} + \text{any CPF shortfall}$.
- **Bank Loan Scenario:** Minimum cash is $\max(\text{COV} + 0.05 \times \text{Valuation}, 0) + \text{cash for fees/BSD if CPF OA exhausted}$.
