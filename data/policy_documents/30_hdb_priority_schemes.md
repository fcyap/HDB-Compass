## Greater priority for first-timer families

First-timer families enjoy greater priority and privileges when they apply for a flat from HDB. Couples comprising a first-timer and a second-timer are treated as a first-timer family and enjoy the same benefits to help them buy their first home as a new family nucleus. Read more about the distribution of BTO flat supply and the additional ballot chances that a first-timer family may receive when applying for a flat during a Build-To-Order (BTO) or Sale of Balance Flats (SBF) exercise.

## Priority schemes for different groups of flat buyers

Priority schemes help eligible flat buyers enhance their chances in the computer ballot for flats offered during BTO and SBF exercises, though they do not guarantee a favourable queue number for flat selection.

You may apply for 1 priority scheme when submitting a flat application, if the core nucleus meets the eligibility conditions when you apply for an HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter. If you qualify for the Family and Parenthood Priority Scheme (FPPS), you may apply for 1 other priority scheme, such as the Family Care Scheme (FCS) (Proximity) or the Third Child Priority Scheme (TCPS).

#### Couples expecting a child and families with child(ren)

The following priority schemes help families with children or expecting a child to get their flat more easily.

#### Parents and child(ren) who wish to live with/ near each other

The following schemes provide priority to parents and children, married or single, who are applying for a new flat to live with or near each other, to strengthen intergenerational family support. Fiancé/ fiancée applicants may also qualify.

#### Seniors who wish to live near their existing home

The following priority scheme provides priority access for seniors looking to buy a 2-room Flexi flat to age-in-place in a familiar environment.

#### Public rental tenants who wish to purchase their own home

The following scheme provides priority to public rental tenants who are ready to own a home.

### Tenants’ Priority Scheme (TPS)

## Flat allocation

Up to 10% of 2-room Flexi and 3-room BTO/ SBF flats are set aside for TPS applicants. This quota is shared with applicants who apply for a flat under the Selective En bloc Redevelopment Scheme (SERS), relocation, resettlement or SLA’s acquisition exercises.

## Eligibility conditions

You and your family must be living in an HDB public rental flat for at least 2 years at the time of your HFE letter application.

### Senior Priority Scheme (SPS) (near existing home)

## Flat allocation

Within the seniors' quota, half in Standard and Plus projects, and a quarter in Prime projects, are set aside for eligible seniors applying under the FCS (Proximity) and SPS (near existing home).

## Eligibility conditions

The 2-room Flexi flat must be within 4 km of the HDB flat or private residential property that you own and live in. The address of this property must also be reflected in your NRIC.

### Third Child Priority Scheme (TCPS)

## Flat allocation

Up to 10% of BTO flats, and up to 10% of SBF flats are set aside for TCPS applicants.

## Eligibility conditions

- You and/ or your spouse is a Singapore Citizen
- If you are divorced or widowed, you must be a Singapore Citizen to qualify
- Your family must consist of at least 3 children from a legal marriage or legally adopted, or are expecting a third child. If the family is expecting\*, you must have a doctor's certificate confirming the pregnancy. Divorced parents must have legal custody, care, and control of their children. Adoptive parents must provide the Adoption Order when applying for an HFE letter.
- Your third child must be born on or after 1 January 1987
- Your youngest child or the child you are expecting must be a Singapore Citizen
- Your other children must be either Singapore Citizens or Singapore Permanent Residents
- You have not previously bought a flat under the TCPS

\*This includes cases where the parent(s) have 1 child and are expecting 2 or more children, or where the parent(s) do not have a child yet and are expecting 3 or more children.

After collecting the keys to your new flat, the 3 children who have helped you to qualify for the TCPS must continue to live with you during the minimum occupation period of the new flat. In addition, they must not:

- Apply or be included as occupiers in an application to buy a flat from HDB or on the open market, or an EC unit from property developer; or
- Rent an HDB flat.

### Assistance Scheme for Second-Timers (Divorced/ Widowed Parents) (ASSIST)

## Flat allocation

Up to 5% of 2-room Flexi flats and up to 10% of 3-room flats in Standard BTO projects are allocated for ASSIST. This is part of the BTO flats in Standard projects set aside for second-timer families, which is up to 15% of the non-senior public supply of 2-room Flexi flats, and up to 20% of the 3-room public flat supply.

## Eligibility conditions

- You must have at least 1 child aged 18 or below (i.e. natural offspring from a legal marriage or legally adopted). For adoptive parents, you must have the Adoption Order of your child when you apply for an HFE letter.
- You must not have acquired any interest in an HDB flat or private residential property, except for your matrimonial flat/ property, after the date of divorce/ separation or demise of your spouse. Read about the eligibility conditions for buying a new flat, including non-ownership of private residential property.

### First-Timer (Parents & Married Couples), or FT(PMC) category

## Flat allocation

First-timer families who meet all the following eligibility criteria may qualify for the FT(PMC) category:

- Families with at least 1 Singapore Citizen child aged 18 and below, or married couples aged 40 and below. This includes married couples who are expecting and have a doctor’s certification of the pregnancy
- Never owned or sold a residential property before, including an HDB flat
- Did not have a chance to book a BTO/ SBF flat in the past 5 years prior to their flat application

You do not need to request to be considered for the FT(PMC) category. HDB will assess and inform you of your eligibility after you submit your flat application.

## Additional ballot chance

You will receive 1 additional ballot chance when applying for a flat in the BTO/ SBF exercises, on top of the 2 ballot chances that you will receive as a first-timer family. This means that you will receive a total of 3 ballot chances when you apply for any flat type in any estate.

### Family and Parenthood Priority Scheme (FPPS)

## Flat allocation

Up to 40% of the public supply for BTO flats and up to 60% of the public supply for SBF flats are set aside for applicants under the FPPS.

## Eligibility conditions

- You and your spouse must be first-timer buyers
- You have a Singapore Citizen child, i.e. natural offspring from a legal marriage or legally adopted, who is aged 18 or below. For adoptive parents, you must have the Adoption Order of your child when you apply for an HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter.
- If you or your spouse is expecting, and have a doctor's certification of the pregnancy, you may also apply for the FPPS

If you qualify for the FT(PMC) category, you will be eligible for the FPPS and enjoy first priority when you apply for a 4-room or smaller BTO flat in Standard projects.

## Balloting process

Flat buyers eligible for the FPPS may also apply for 1 other priority scheme, such as the TCPS. A computerised ballot is conducted for each priority scheme. For example, for a first-timer family with 3 children who is eligible for both the TCPS and FPPS, their flat application will first be balloted under the TCPS. If the ballot is unsuccessful, their flat application is balloted again under the FPPS.

### Family Care Scheme (FCS) (Proximity)

## Flat allocation

Parents and their child(ren), married or single, can enjoy priority access when applying to live with or near each other under the FCS (Proximity). This scheme replaces the previous Married Child Priority Scheme (MCPS) and the Senior Priority Scheme (SPS) (living with or near parents/ married child).

The following flat allocation quotas are set aside under this scheme:

- Seniors: Within the seniors' quota, half in Standard and Plus Projects, and a quarter in Prime projects, are set aside for seniors applying under the FCS (Proximity) or SPS (near existing home).

- First-timer families: Up to 30% of the public supply for BTO and SBF flats
- Second-timer families: Up to 5% of the public supply for BTO flats and up to 3% of the public supply for SBF flats
- First-timer singles: Within the non-senior public supply of 2-room Flexi flats, up to 30% for BTO flats and up to 2% for SBF flats.

## Eligibility conditions

- At least 1 parent/ child, married or single (including widowed or divorced parent/ child), is a Singapore Citizen or Singapore Permanent Resident. The single child must be aged 35 years old and above.
- If you are applying to live with your parents/ child, you must include them in your HFE letter and flat application. They must physically reside in the flat and their names cannot be removed during the minimum occupation period (MOP) after the flat purchase. If you or any person listed in the application owns or has an interest in any HDB flat, you must dispose of the interest in the flat, within 6 months of completing your flat purchase.
- If you are applying to live near your parents/ child, you must be applying for a flat in a project where the nearest block is within 4 km from an HDB flat, HDB rental flat, or a private residential property that your parents/ child lives in:

### Family Care Scheme (FCS) (Joint Balloting)

## Flat allocation

Parents and their child(ren), married or single, can jointly apply for 2 units in the same BTO project, where there are 2-room Flexi or 3-room flats offered, under the FCS (Joint Balloting). This scheme replaces the previous Multi-Generation Priority Scheme (MGPS).

The following flat allocation quotas are set aside under this scheme:

- Parents: Up to 15% of the 2-room Flexi and 3-room flats, subject to availability of flat type.
- Child (married or single): Up to 15% of 2-room Flexi to 5-room flats, subject to availability of flat type.

## Eligibility conditions

- Parents (including widowed or divorced parents) must submit a joint application with their child. If the child is widowed or divorced and has child(ren) under his/ her legal custody, care, and control, he/ she will also qualify for FCS (Joint Balloting).
- Parents may only apply for a 2-room Flexi (including short lease flats) or 3-room flat.
- The child, if single, must be a first-timer aged 35 and above, and applying for a 2-room Flexi flat. If married, the household must be a first- or second-timer family and applying up to a 5-room flat.
- If more than 1 project is offered in the same town, successful parent and child applicants must book flats in the same project.

## Balloting process

If your FCS (Joint Balloting) applications are successful, you and your parents/ child will each receive a queue position within the respective flat supplies. HDB will schedule flat booking appointments based on your respective queue positions, which may take place separately.

| Applicants | Scheme | Number of Queue Position Issued |
|---|---|---|
| Parents | FCS (Joint Balloting) | 1 queue position for parents |
| Child (married  or single) |  | 1 queue position for child |

If you do not book a flat when units are available, you will incur a non-selection count. Both the parents' and child's applications under the FCS (Joint Balloting), as well as all ongoing flat application(s), will be cancelled. Nevertheless, there may be situations when flat applicants are left with limited choices when they are due to book a flat. HDB will waive the non-selection count for applicants with 10 or fewer BTO flats to choose from when they are due to book a flat.

If your flat applications under FCS (Joint Balloting) are unsuccessful, you and your parents’/ child’s flat applications will be separately balloted under the Public Scheme.
