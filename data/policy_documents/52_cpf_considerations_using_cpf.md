## Considerations when using CPF to buy property

**Notice**

Before buying a home, consider how it will impact your lifestyle and finances over time. To be prudent, keep your total debt servicing ratio and your monthly housing instalment within 55% and 25% of your gross monthly income respectively. Factors like household size, mortgage repayment ability and your retirement plan should also be considered.

**Eligibility**

Check with HDB

**Maximum loan amount**

Up to 75% of purchase price, subject to terms and conditions

**Interest rate**

Fixed rate, pegged at 0.1% above CPF OA interest rate

**Capital repayment**

HDB allows capital repayment

**Adjustment to monthly repayment amount**

HDB allows adjustments

**Eligibility**

Check with bank

**Maximum loan amount**

Up to 75% of purchase price, subject to terms and conditions

**Interest rate**

Differs across banks

**Capital repayment**

Subject to financier’s approval and terms and conditions

**Adjustment to monthly repayment amount**

Subject to financier’s approval and terms and conditions

Before you decide whether to use cash or your OA savings to buy a property, it is important to plan with your retirement needs in mind. Once you have used more of your OA savings for your property, keep in mind that you’ll have less CPF savings left for retirement.

As you begin planning for your new home, start by calculating the amount of OA savings you can use for your property purchase. Use the CPF housing usage calculator to estimate the amount of OA savings you can use.

If you have an existing property, log in to Home ownership dashboard to check the amount used for your property and how much more you can use before reaching the usage limits where applicable.

We recommend you retain $20,000 in your OA as it can act as an emergency fund, while earning a risk-free interest of up to 3.5%\*.

In the case of any unexpected scenarios such as a sudden loss of income, you can use it to continue paying for your monthly instalments, minimising disruptions to your housing loan payments and providing you with peace of mind. OA savings not utilised can boost your retirement payouts in future.

\*Members will earn an extra interest of 1% per annum on the first $60,000 of combined CPF balances (capped at $20,000 for OA). Extra interest earned from OA balances will go into your SA.
