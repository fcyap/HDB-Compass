## 1. Annual BTO Launch Cadence

Beginning in **2024**, HDB consolidated its Build-To-Order (BTO) sales exercises from four quarterly launches into **three larger launches per year**:
- **February Launch**
- **June Launch**
- **October Launch**

Consolidating the launches allows HDB to offer larger flat supplies with broader geographical variety per exercise, giving applicants greater choice and reducing application fragmentation. Concurrently, Sale of Balance Flats (SBF) exercises are offered alongside designated BTO launches to redistribute unsold or surrendered units.

## 2. Priority Schemes & Ballot Quotas

Because demand for BTO flats regularly exceeds supply in popular towns, HDB allocates specific percentages of flat supply to prioritized applicant categories through dedicated statutory priority schemes.

### 2.1 First-Timer (Parents & Married Couples) — FT-PMC Priority
Introduced in late 2023 to provide urgent housing support to young families and married couples:
- **Eligibility Criteria:**
  1. Married couples with at least one Singapore Citizen child aged **18 and below**; OR
  2. Married couples aged **40 and below** who are both first-time buyers.
  3. Must not have owned or sold any residential property within the past 30 months.
- **Enhanced Ballot Chances:** FT-PMC applicants receive **3 ballot chances** in the computerized balloting system (compared to 2 chances for standard first-timers and 1 chance for second-timers).
- **Dedicated Quota:** Up to **40% of the 3-room BTO supply** and up to **40% of the 4-room and 5-room BTO supply** are exclusively reserved for FT-PMC applicants across all launches.

### 2.2 Married Child Priority Scheme (MCPS)
The MCPS helps parents and married children live with or near each other for mutual caregiving:
- **Spatial Requirement:** The applicant must apply for a BTO flat in the **same town** or within **4 km** of their parents' or married child's residential address.
- **Quota Allocation:**
  - Up to **30% of the BTO flat supply** for first-timer families.
  - Up to **3% of the BTO flat supply** for second-timer families.
- **Living Together Priority:** Within the MCPS quota, first priority is granted to applicants who apply to physically live together with their parents in the same flat.

### 2.3 Multi-Generation Priority Scheme (MGPS)
- **Concept:** Enables parents and their married child to submit a joint application for two flats in the **same BTO project**.
- **Flat Types:**
  - Parents apply for a **2-room Flexi or 3-room flat**.
  - Married child applies for a **2-room Flexi, 3-room, 4-room, or 5-room flat**.
- **Quota Allocation:** Up to **15% of 2-room Flexi / 3-room flats** in the project are reserved for parents, and an equivalent number of flats for the married child. Both parties receive integrated queue numbers to choose adjoining or neighbouring units.

### 2.4 Parenthood Priority Scheme (PPS)
- **Eligibility:** First-timer married couples who are expecting a child (doctor's certification required) or who have at least one Singapore Citizen child aged 18 and below.
- **Quota Allocation:** Up to **30% of BTO flat supply** is allocated to PPS families (nested within the FT-PMC allocation).

### 2.5 Senior Priority Scheme (SPS)
- **Target Demographic:** Seniors aged **55 and above** who wish to right-size into a 2-room Flexi flat.
- **Proximity Requirement:** Applies if buying a 2-room Flexi flat near existing residence or within 4 km of married children.
- **Quota Allocation:** At least **40% of 2-room Flexi flats** in each BTO project are statutorily reserved for seniors under the SPS.

### 2.6 Third-Child Priority Scheme (TCPS)
- **Eligibility:** Families with at least three children (at least one must be a Singapore Citizen born after 1 Jan 1987).
- **Quota Allocation:** Up to **5% of BTO flat supply** is reserved for TCPS applicants.

## 3. Strict Non-Selection Rules & Penalties

To deter speculative applications and ensure queue numbers are utilized by genuine buyers, HDB tightened non-selection penalties:

### 3.1 The Rule
- If an applicant is successfully balloted and issued a **queue number that falls within the available flat supply**, but **declines or fails to select a flat** during their flat booking appointment, they are recorded as having forfeited their booking opportunity.
- **First-Timer Penalty:** Any first-timer applicant who declines to select a flat when invited will **lose their first-timer priority status for one full year** in subsequent BTO balloting exercises (relegated to second-timer status with only 1 ballot chance and lower quota access).
- **Extenuating Circumstances:** HDB only waives the non-selection penalty if fewer than 10 flats (or fewer than 10% of total supply) remain available in the applicant's preferred flat category at the point of their booking appointment.
