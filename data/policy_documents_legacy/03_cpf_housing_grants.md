## 1. Overview of CPF Housing Grants

The Singapore Government provides extensive, progressive housing subsidies to Singapore Citizens purchasing public housing. Grants are disbursed directly into the eligible applicants' **CPF Ordinary Account (CPF OA)**. 

### 1.1 How Grant Funds Are Used
- **Purchase Price Offset:** Grant monies are applied directly to reduce the net flat purchase price or the required mortgage loan quantum.
- **Downpayment Funding:** Grant disbursements can be used directly to satisfy the 25% purchase downpayment requirement.
- **No Cash Disbursements:** Housing grants are never paid out as liquid cash to the buyer.
- **Resale Refund Obligations:** When the flat is eventually sold on the open market, the principal grant amount plus **2.5% compounded annual accrued interest** must be refunded back into the seller's CPF OA. However, under HDB's statutory protection rule, if the flat is sold at or above official market valuation and the net sales proceeds (after repaying the outstanding mortgage) are insufficient to cover the full grant + accrued interest, the seller is **not required to top up the remaining shortfall in cash**.

## 2. Enhanced CPF Housing Grant (EHG)

The Enhanced CPF Housing Grant (EHG) is the primary progressive subsidy for lower-to-middle income Singaporeans. On **20 August 2024**, MND and HDB significantly raised the maximum grant amounts across the board to strengthen support for first-time homebuyers.

### 2.1 EHG for First-Timer Families
- **Maximum Grant Quantum:** Raised to **$120,000** (previously $80,000).
- **Monthly Household Income Ceiling:** Average gross monthly household income over the preceding 12 months must not exceed **$9,000**.
- **Continuous Employment Requirement:** At least one of the core applicants must have worked continuously for at least **12 months** prior to the flat application, and remain employed at the time of application.
- **Coverage for Both BTO and Resale:** Eligible first-timer families can use the EHG for new BTO flats (Standard, Plus, Prime) as well as open-market resale flats.

### 2.3 The 95-Year Lease Coverage Rule & Pro-Rating Formula
To ensure Singaporeans buy homes that provide security for their full lifetime:
1. **Full Grant Condition:** The flat's remaining lease must be sufficient to cover the **youngest buyer to at least 95 years of age**.
2. **Pro-Rating Rule:** If the remaining lease is insufficient to cover the youngest buyer to age 95, the grant amount is pro-rated according to the ratio of actual lease coverage to required coverage:
   $$\text{Disbursed Grant} = \text{Full Statutory Grant} \times \left( \frac{\text{Remaining Lease Years of Flat}}{95 - \text{Age of Youngest Buyer}} \right)$$
3. **Minimum Absolute Lease:** The flat must have at least **20 years of remaining lease** to qualify for any CPF Housing Grant. If remaining lease is under 20 years, zero grant is disbursed.

## 3. CPF Family Grant (Resale Flats Only)

The CPF Family Grant provides substantial financial assistance specifically for first-timer married couples or families buying a secondary **resale flat** on the open market.

### 3.1 Eligibility Requirements
- **First-Timer Status:** Neither applicant nor spouse must have received any previous housing subsidy or grant.
- **Citizenship Pairings:** At least one Singapore Citizen (SC) applicant plus one SC or Singapore Permanent Resident (PR) spouse/core member.
- **Income Ceiling:** Gross monthly household income must not exceed **$14,000** (or **$21,000** for multi-generation/extended family applications).

### 3.2 Family Grant Tiers by Flat Size and Citizenship
| Household Citizenship Structure | Flat Type: 2-Room to 4-Room Resale | Flat Type: 5-Room or Larger Resale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SC + SC Couple (Both Singapore Citizens)** | **$80,000** | **$50,000** |
| **SC + PR Couple (Citizen + Permanent Resident)** | **$70,000** | **$40,000** |

#### The $10,000 Citizen Top-Up
For SC/PR couples who received $70,000 (or $40,000), they are entitled to an additional **$10,000 Citizen Top-Up** if:
1. The PR spouse subsequently acquires Singapore Citizenship; OR
2. The couple gives birth to or adopts a child who is a Singapore Citizen.
Application for the Citizen Top-Up can be submitted at any time while the flat is still owned by the couple.

## 4. Singles Grant for Resale Flats

For single citizens aged 35 and above buying an HDB resale flat on the open market:
- **Eligible Flat Types:** 2-room to 5-room Standard resale flats, or 2-room Plus/Prime resale flats.
- **Monthly Income Ceiling:** Gross monthly income not exceeding **$7,000**.
- **Grant Amounts:**
  - **2-room to 4-room Resale Flat:** **$40,000**
  - **5-room Resale Flat:** **$25,000**

## 5. Proximity Housing Grant (PHG) (Resale Flats Only)

The Proximity Housing Grant (PHG) is designed to encourage intergenerational mutual care and support by helping families live with or close to their parents or married children.

### 5.1 Distinctive Statutory Characteristics
- **No Income Ceiling:** The PHG has **zero household income ceiling**. High-income and middle-income families qualify equally.
- **Repeat Buyers Eligible:** Available to **both first-timers and second-timers**. Previous housing grant recipients can still claim the PHG.
- **Resale Only:** Applies exclusively to open-market resale flats (not new BTO flats).

### 5.2 Distance Requirement & Spatial Boundary
- **Proximity Threshold:** The flat being purchased must be within a **strict 4 km radius** from the parents' or children's registered HDB flat or private residential address.
- **Measurement:** HDB calculates proximity using straight-line Euclidean distance between building postal centroids.

### 5.3 PHG Grant Quantum Table
| Household Structure | Living With Parents / Child (Same Flat) | Living Near Parents / Child (Within 4 km) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Families / Married Couples** | **$30,000** | **$20,000** |
| **Singles (Aged 35 and above)** | **$15,000** | **$10,000** |

## 6. Grant Stacking Architecture & Total Subsidy Calculations

Eligible first-timer buyers purchasing a resale flat are legally entitled to **stack all three grants simultaneously**, dramatically reducing their required cash and mortgage outlays.

### 6.1 Maximum Grant Stacking: First-Timer Family Scenario
Consider a young Singaporean married couple (SC/SC, both first-timers) earning a combined income of $1,500/month, purchasing a 4-room resale flat within 4 km of the wife's parents:
1. **Enhanced CPF Housing Grant (EHG):** **$120,000**
2. **CPF Family Grant:** **$80,000**
3. **Proximity Housing Grant (PHG):** **$30,000** (if living together) or **$20,000** (within 4 km)
- **Maximum Cumulative Subsidy:** Up to **$230,000** in direct grants.

### 6.2 Maximum Grant Stacking: First-Timer Single Citizen Scenario
Consider an eligible 35-year-old single citizen earning $750/month buying a 4-room resale flat within 4 km of parents:
1. **Enhanced CPF Housing Grant (Singles EHG):** **$60,000**
2. **Singles Grant:** **$40,000**
3. **Proximity Housing Grant (Singles PHG):** **$15,000** (with parents) or **$10,000** (within 4 km)
- **Maximum Cumulative Subsidy:** Up to **$115,000** in direct grants.

### 6.3 Step-Up Housing Grant (Second-Timer Assistance)
- **Quantum:** **$15,000**
- **Target Group:** Lower-income second-timer families currently living in a 2-room subsidised flat in a non-mature estate who wish to right-size or upgrade to a 3-room standard flat.
