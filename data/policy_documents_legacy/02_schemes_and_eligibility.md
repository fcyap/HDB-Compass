## 1. Statutory Housing Eligibility Schemes

To purchase a new Build-To-Order (BTO) flat or a secondary resale flat, applicants must apply under an established statutory HDB eligibility scheme. Each scheme enforces distinct family nucleus structures, age thresholds, citizenship pairings, and income ceilings.

### 1.1 Public Scheme
- **Core Requirement:** Forms a traditional family nucleus comprising at least one Singapore Citizen (SC) applicant and at least one other qualifying family member who is an SC or Singapore Permanent Resident (PR).
- **Recognised Family Nuclei:**
  1. Applicant, spouse, and children (if any).
  2. Applicant, parents, and siblings (if any).
  3. Applicant and children under applicant's legal custody, care, and control (for widowed or divorced parents).
- **Income Ceilings (New BTO):**
  - General 4-room or larger BTO flats: **$14,000/month** gross household income.
  - Multi-generational or extended family: **$21,000/month**.
  - 3-room BTO flats (select projects): **$7,000/month** or **$14,000/month** depending on project terms.
- **Flat Size Entitlement:** Eligible for all flat types across Standard, Plus, and Prime launches, and all resale flat types islandwide.

### 1.2 Fiancé / Fiancée Scheme
- **Core Requirement:** Designed for couples preparing for marriage. Consists of an SC applicant and an SC or PR fiancé/fiancée.
- **Minimum Age:** Both parties must be at least **21 years old**.
- **Special Conditions:**
  - Applicants aged between 18 and 20 must obtain written parental/guardian consent.
  - Applicants below 18 are ineligible.
- **Marriage Registration Requirement:** The couple must solemnise their marriage and submit their official Marriage Certificate to HDB **within 3 months of taking physical possession (key collection)** of the flat.
- **Grant Timing:** Couples eligible for the Enhanced CPF Housing Grant (EHG) or Family Grant receive the grant upon flat booking, but failure to produce the marriage certificate within the stipulated timeframe results in flat repossession and clawback of grant disbursements.

### 1.3 Single Singapore Citizen (SSC) Scheme
- **Minimum Age Requirement:** The applicant must be a Singapore Citizen aged **35 and above** (unmarried, widowed, or legally divorced).
- **The Landmark October 2024 Policy Overhaul:**
  - Prior to October 2024, singles could only purchase 2-room Flexi BTO flats in designated "Non-Mature" estates.
  - Under the revised framework effective October 2024, eligible singles aged 35+ can purchase **2-room Flexi BTO flats across ALL locations and classification tiers islandwide** (Standard, Plus, and Prime).
- **BTO Flat Size Restriction:** Singles applying under the SSC scheme are strictly limited to **2-room Flexi BTO flats**. They cannot apply for 3-room, 4-room, or 5-room new flats.
- **BTO Income Ceiling:** Average gross monthly income must not exceed **$7,000/month**.
- **Resale Flat Entitlements for Singles:**
  - **Standard Resale Flats:** Singles aged 35+ can purchase **any size flat on the open market** (2-room, 3-room, 4-room, 5-room, or Executive). There is **no income ceiling** to buy a Standard resale flat on the open market unless applying for CPF Housing Grants (where the income ceiling is $4,500/month for the Singles EHG and $7,000/month for the Singles Grant).
  - **Plus & Prime Resale Flats:** Singles aged 35+ are strictly restricted to **2-room resale flats only**, and must not exceed the **$7,000/month** income ceiling.

### 1.4 Joint Singles Scheme (JSS) & JSS Operator-Run Pilot
- **Standard JSS:** Allows **2 to 4 unmarried Singapore Citizens**, all aged **35 and above**, to co-purchase a residential property together.
  - Eligible for a 2-room Flexi BTO flat across Standard, Plus, and Prime tiers.
  - Combined gross monthly household income must not exceed **$14,000/month** for BTO or Plus/Prime resale flats.
  - On the open resale market, JSS applicants can purchase any size Standard flat with no income ceiling.
- **JSS Operator-Run (JSS-OR) Pilot:** A social assistance model where low-income singles who do not possess the capital to purchase a flat can rent an individual bedroom in designated partitioned rental flats managed by community operators.

### 1.5 Non-Citizen Spouse Scheme (NCSS) & Non-Citizen Family Scheme
- **Eligibility:** Designed for a Singapore Citizen (aged 21+) married to a non-citizen spouse holding a valid Long-Term Visit Pass (LTVP) or Work Pass.
- **Resale Market Access:** The citizen applicant can buy any flat size on the open resale market under the Single Singapore Citizen Scheme (if aged 35+) or under the Non-Citizen Spouse Scheme (if aged 21+, eligible for 2-room to 5-room Standard resale flats).
- **New BTO Access:** Eligible to apply for 2-room Flexi BTO flats in Standard estates if aged 35 and above.

### 1.6 Assistance Scheme for Second-Timers (Divorced / Widowed Parents) — ASSIST
- **Eligibility:** Divorced or widowed Singapore Citizens aged 21 and above with at least one child aged 18 and below from the dissolved marriage.
- **Quota Protection:** Up to **5% of 2-room Flexi and 3-room BTO flats** across BTO launches are legally reserved for this vulnerable group to ensure rapid re-housing.

## 2. Private Property Wait-Out Regulations & Exemptions

To ensure that public housing remains prioritized for citizens with genuine owner-occupier housing needs, Singapore enforces strict cooling measures and wait-out periods for current and former private residential property owners (PPOs).

### 2.1 The 15-Month Wait-Out Period for Open Market Resale Flats
- **Policy Origin:** Introduced on **30 September 2022** as a macroprudential demand-moderation measure.
- **The Rule:** Any individual who currently owns, or has owned, transferred, or disposed of any interest in private residential property (including private condominiums, landed properties, HUDC units, or executive condominiums) must wait **15 months** from the legal date of disposal before they are eligible to purchase a **non-subsidised HDB resale flat**.
- **Scope:** The wait-out period applies to all buyers below 55 years of age.

### 2.2 Crucial Exemption: Senior Citizens (Aged 55+) Right-Sizing
A critical, high-frequency policy exemption exists to facilitate retirement planning and right-sizing for elderly Singaporeans:
- **Exemption Rule:** Seniors aged **55 and above** (and their spouses) who are right-sizing from a private property to an HDB resale flat are **completely and strictly EXEMPT from the 15-month wait-out period**.
- **Qualifying Flat Types:** The exemption applies exclusively when purchasing a **4-room or smaller resale flat** (i.e., 2-room, 3-room, or 4-room flat).
- **Ineligible Flat Types (Full Wait-Out Applies):** If a senior aged 55+ attempts to purchase a **5-room flat, 3Gen flat, Executive Apartment, or Executive Maisonette**, the exemption does NOT apply. They must observe the full **15-month wait-out period**.
- **Immediate Buying Ability:** Qualifying seniors can buy an Option to Purchase (OTP) for a 4-room or smaller flat immediately upon or after the sale of their private property.

### 2.3 The 30-Month Wait-Out Period
A more stringent **30-month wait-out period** from the disposal of private residential property applies to:
1. Applying for a brand-new Build-To-Order (BTO) flat.
2. Applying for a new Executive Condominium (EC) from a property developer.
3. Applying for CPF Housing Grants (such as the Enhanced CPF Housing Grant, Family Grant, or Singles Grant) when buying a resale flat.
4. Purchasing a **Plus or Prime flat** on the secondary resale market.

### 2.4 Commercial and Industrial Properties
- Current or past ownership of purely **commercial property** (e.g., retail shops, commercial offices, shophouses without residential living quarters) or **industrial property** (e.g., B1/B2 factory units) **does NOT trigger the 15-month residential wait-out period**.
- The buyer is permitted to buy a resale HDB flat while retaining ownership of the commercial/industrial property, provided they physically reside in the HDB flat as an owner-occupier.
